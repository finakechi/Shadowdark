# Project status

Last updated: 2026-10-24 (UTC)

## Objective and stage

Develop a short underground mini-campaign for approximately three players: at least four sessions, but not many more. Start at level 1, with expanded character options and revised exploration flow to be designed. Tone references: Elden Ring, The Witcher and Forgotten Realms. Plan tentatively for virtual play. Sessions last 3–4 hours. Actual premise/adventure, platform and exact homebrew remain open. More magic and less exploration bookkeeping are explicit priorities; largely preserve torchlight pressure and require serious costs for major magical obstacle bypasses. Greater toughness is under consideration, not mechanically adopted. Current focus: adapt the twelve 2024 Player's Handbook class identities to this homebrew. The [complete-kit evaluation](docs/characters/class-kit-evaluation.md) now covers all twelve; exact class sheets remain to be written.
