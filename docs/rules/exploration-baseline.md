## Implications for this project — interpretation

The published procedure already offers more flexibility than mandatory individual declarations in an unchanging order. A rules explanation should not portray simultaneous actions or normal group movement as inventions of our homebrew.

The remaining structure still counts a completed party round. Removing only strict order preserves encounter cadence and spell timing, but may leave the bookkeeping Noah dislikes. Removing round counting also changes those connected systems; a replacement must specify what happens to wandering encounters and short-duration effects.

The book also already expects information to be obtainable through sensible investigation. Adding a roll or a separate administrative turn for every minor observation would make our version slower and is not required by the cited baseline.

Preserving the torch countdown alone does not preserve all baseline challenge. The rate at which a group completes crawling rounds determines how many baseline encounter checks occur per real hour. There is no fixed conversion from those rounds to a real-time check schedule without observing play.

## Guardrails for a proposal

- Separate the light clock, wandering encounter cadence and round-sensitive effects.
- Preserve scarce light, declared time costs and concrete consequences for lingering.
- Do not silently give a one-round spell a ten-minute duration.
- Keep rest and overland procedures distinct from minute-scale exploration.
- Identify every replaced rule, including the published time-skip encounter check.
- Preserve broad class expertise and informative investigation rather than requiring repeated dice rolls.
- Evaluate spotlight, not only speed, when replacing individual turns.
