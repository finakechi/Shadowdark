# Balance and playtest notes

No sessions or experiments recorded as of 2026-10-23. Hypotheses are not results.

## Minimum useful evidence

Record session/date, participant count without private identities, character levels/classes and relevant sources, exact rules/HB version, scenario, available resources, objective and major dice/ruling anomalies.

Observe:
- Meaningful choices made or avoided; any dominant repeatable option.
- Combat damage, incapacitations, enemy opportunities and retreat.
- Noncombat obstacles resolved, bypassed or made irrelevant.
- Light, consumables, spell availability and recovery pressure.
- Contribution of each class; any displaced role.
- Time spent deciding, resolving or looking up rules.
- GM preparation and adjudication burden.
- Player reports of frustration, clarity and enjoyment.

Compare baseline and variant under reasonably similar conditions, stating what differed. Preserve successful tactics as successes; do not treat every easy encounter as imbalance. Small samples and swingy dice require tentative conclusions. Avoid precision beyond the evidence.

## Review outcome

Hypothesis; observed behavior; evidence quality; confounders; score changes; recommendation; Noah's decision; next test. Link to the authoritative HB proposal and SD decision instead of repeating accepted rules.
