Status: design workspace; no encounters or advancement rules approved.

## Before designing

Confirm party size, starting level, actual abilities, rule versions, resources, lethality and objective. Consult the [baseline report](../rules/rules-reference.md). Do not import another game's challenge-rating assumptions.

## Reward and progression checks

Treasure incentives should support the stated objective and plausible exploration choices. Check actual XP thresholds and carousing rules before promising advancement. Consider the utility and obstacle-bypassing effects of items as well as damage/armor. New items should have original wording, bounded effects and clear usage costs.

Do not counter every new player capability. Identify which challenges it resolves and provide other meaningful pressures. Assess original monsters' number of actions and control effects as carefully as HP and damage. Record actual results in [playtesting](../playtesting/balance-notes.md).
