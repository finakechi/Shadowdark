## Highest-value follow-up

1. **Session length:** Roughly 2–3, 3–4, or 4–5 hours? This determines how much underground content fits.
2. **Capability target:** Does D&D 2024-like capability mainly mean more distinctive features/choices, or also similar starting toughness and recovery? Prefer (A) more options with Shadowdark-like fragility, (B) more options plus a modest survival buffer, or (C) substantially closer D&D-like toughness? These are choices to clarify, not packages already adopted. Action economy is a separate question; no imported bonus-action/reaction system assumed.
3. **Exploration friction:** Was the main problem waiting through fixed turn order, feeling limited to one small task, repeated procedural bookkeeping, or having few interesting abilities to use? Multiple causes are possible. Compare free conversation with periodic group time checks against simultaneous exploration declarations with spotlight tracking.
