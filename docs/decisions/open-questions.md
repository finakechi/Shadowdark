# Campaign setup questions

Open as of 2026-10-23. No answers inferred. Essential means needed to finalize playable prep; research and organization can continue.

## Table and schedule — essential

1. **Q01 — Players and experience:** How many players are likely, and how much RPG experience do they have? A tentative range is enough.
2. **Q02 — GM baseline:** What have you read or run of Shadowdark, which other RPGs have you GMed, and which Shadowdark products do you already own? This determines onboarding and avoids duplicate purchases.
3. **Q03 — Scope and time:** Which works best: one session (fastest trial), 2–4 sessions (room for advancement and retesting; recommended for your design goals if scheduling permits), or undecided? How long is each session, and is there a target date?

## Experience and risk — essential

4. **Q04 — Tone and boundaries:** Do you prefer grim sword-and-sorcery, eerie adventure with humor, or pulpy heroism with danger? What themes should be excluded or kept offscreen? Give private answers privately; do not copy personal boundaries into the public repo.
5. **Q05 — Play emphasis:** Rank exploration, combat, social play and puzzles. For shape, choose dungeon-focused (clearest learning baseline), mixed expedition (broader utility), or faction/story-led (more social preparation).
6. **Q06 — Lethality:** Choose standard dangerous play (retreat matters; death possible), dangerous but buffered (one agreed rescue/recovery mechanism), or low-death/high-consequence (capture, debts or losses carry more weight). No option is adopted automatically.

## Characters and rules — can defer until adventure selection, except Q09

7. **Q07 — Starting capability:** Prefer level 1 to learn growth, level 2–3 for more developed characters, or an explicit level-0 gauntlet? What progression pace would feel rewarding?
8. **Q08 — Character direction:** Prefer fast pregens, normal randomized creation, or guided choices for a character concept? Choice can reduce unwanted randomness but may increase creation time and specialization.
9. **Q09 — Homebrew targets:** Which one or two frustrations matter most: random creation/progression, limited martial options, fragile characters, spell reliability, noncombat utility, or something else? Would you try one short baseline session or prefer one targeted change immediately?

## Materials and logistics — Q10 essential; Q11 can defer

10. **Q10 — Adventure approach:** Prefer official adventure (less prep and a useful baseline), original adventure (maximum premise control), or an official framework with original hooks (recommended compromise)? Are any products or settings particularly appealing?
11. **Q11 — Play format:** In person, virtual or hybrid? Which tools do you use, and do you want printable sheets, a shared map, or a VTT? We can postpone platform-specific assets until this is settled.

## Answer handling

Short answers numbered Q01–Q11 are sufficient; partial answers are useful. Record only consented, non-sensitive campaign choices in the decision log; player identities, schedules with personal details, private boundaries and contact data stay in permissioned storage. Change these questions to links to decisions as they are resolved rather than maintaining a competing answer sheet.
