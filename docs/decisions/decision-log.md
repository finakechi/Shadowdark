Status: Accepted  
Date: 2026-10-23  
Category: Design direction  
Authority: Noah's startup instructions, section 6.

Decision: Favor worthwhile increases to player power, flexibility and utility while preserving meaningful challenge. Prefer meaningful choices over unconditional numerical increases. This accepts a design objective, not any specific rule.

## SD-012 — Low-bookkeeping exploration and consequential magic

Status: Accepted  
Date: 2026-10-23  
Category: Design constraints  
Authority: Noah's follow-up answers 2–3.

Decision:
- Increase features, meaningful choices and magic.
- Explore greater, potentially D&D-like character toughness with enemies and hazards capable of maintaining danger. Toughness is an option to evaluate, not an adopted HP, healing or damage rule.
- Reduce exploration bookkeeping; tracking too much for characters with few worthwhile actions was the main reported frustration.
- Preserve torchlight pressure for the most part; no exact timer or time procedure has been adopted.
- Avoid inexpensive immediate answers to major problems such as curse removal and comprehensive language comprehension. Such options may exist if they have a serious cost. The named spell examples are design references, not claims about their presence or exact effects in the Quickstart.

Reasoning: Supports capable characters and a more magical experience without routinely removing exploration obstacles or recreating administrative burden.

Alternatives: Sparse low-magic characters; unmodified exploration turns; broadly available obstacle bypasses; automatic toughness increases without threat review.

Tradeoffs: More abilities can add tracking. More toughness plus equal proportional enemy damage may preserve the same error margin rather than improve it. Costs must matter in actual play; cosmetic prices, irrelevant delays or easily replenished charges may not suffice.

Dependencies: SD-002, SD-004, SD-007, SD-009 and source-backed baseline review.

Next Action: Compare a streamlined exploration procedure, feature/ability structures, durability approaches and a small set of serious-cost utility examples. Use the rubric for concrete proposals; no mechanics become Testing or Accepted through this direction alone.

## SD-014 — Exploration procedure recommendation

Status: Proposed  
Date: 2026-10-23  
Category: Exploration homebrew  
Authority: Assistant recommendation, not Noah's acceptance.

Decision: Consider [HB-001 v0.1](../rules/homebrew/hb-001-exploration-flow.md): conversational exploration, shared real light, an automatic ten-minute GM pressure reminder, provisional danger thresholds and explicit exceptions for time skips, darkness, spells and urgent rounds.

Reasoning: Directly tests lower routine bookkeeping while retaining light pressure. [Verified baseline](../rules/exploration-baseline.md) confirms that official freeform already offers loose order; the material change here is removing routine round counting and replacing encounter timing.

Alternatives: Strict published turns; published freeform with normal round tracking (strongest fallback); group actions counted as crawling rounds.

Tradeoffs: Wandering encounter frequency is changed and not empirically calibrated. Short effects, darkness and split groups still require some counting. Weighted scores are design judgments; the recommended proposal has only a small scoring edge over official freeform.

Dependencies: Noah's choice before Testing; active light/reminder handling; source-backed short effect and rest procedures.

Next Action: Review or revise HB-001, then record any explicit selection for Testing. No test or adopted rule is implied by publishing this proposal.

## SD-015 — Combat initiative and situational noncombat rounds

Status: Accepted  
Date: 2026-10-23  
Category: Play-mode design direction  
Authority: Noah's explicit clarification.

Decision: Combat can retain turn-based initiative. Ordinary out-of-combat play should not run continuously in rounds. Turns/rounds outside combat should serve specific uses, especially immediate dangers where order matters. When light fails in a dangerous area, immediately use urgent crawling rounds and the published darkness restrictions and encounter cadence; do not wait for a ten-minute reminder. Return to freeform exploration after the urgent situation is resolved.

Reasoning: Retains useful timing structure without making it the administrative default for all exploration.

Alternatives: Continuous crawling rounds or entirely unstructured timing even during immediate danger.

Tradeoffs: The GM must identify and communicate why an urgent sequence begins and when it ends. Do not label all tasks urgent or let an effect unnecessarily impose rounds on unrelated activity.

Dependencies: SD-012 and SD-013; exact short-effect handling will be reviewed during abilities/magic design.

Next Action: Apply this architecture to HB-001. Its encounter thresholds, interval and full-package status remain Proposed; the clarification is not blanket acceptance or authorization of Testing.

## SD-018 — Starting durability and encounter calibration

Status: Proposed  
Date: 2026-10-23  
Category: Durability and encounters  
Authority: Conditional assistant recommendation after character/magic comparisons.

Decision: Consider [HB-004](../rules/homebrew/hb-004-durability-and-encounters.md): maximum first class die + 2 + CON, with stated ancestry handling, unchanged later gains/recovery initially, and encounter calibration from actual party capabilities.

Reasoning: A simple first-level buffer may support new choices without a new recovery tracker.

Alternatives: Baseline rolled HP; maximum first die only; doubled baseline starting HP.

Tradeoffs: Proportional gains favor small HP dice; dwarf's first-level rolling advantage becomes inapplicable under maximization. Encounter attrition and healing interactions remain untested. No universal enemy multiplier follows.

Dependencies: Selected abilities/magic, ancestry, actual character sheets and encounter cadence.

Next Action: Choose or revise a durability option after the ability/magic direction is clearer; then review representative encounters. No HP change or revised encounter statistics adopted.
