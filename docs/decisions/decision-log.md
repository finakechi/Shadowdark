## SD-002 — Player options and meaningful challenge

Status: Accepted  
Date: 2026-10-23  
Category: Design direction  
Authority: Noah's startup instructions, section 6.

Decision: Favor worthwhile increases to player power, flexibility and utility while preserving meaningful challenge. Prefer meaningful choices over unconditional numerical increases. This accepts a design objective, not any specific rule.

Reasoning: Supports stronger or more versatile characters without assuming every existing obstacle stays equally difficult.

Alternatives: Unmodified play only; indiscriminate power increases; purely numerical encounter escalation.

Tradeoffs: Some existing challenges will become easier; investigate specific effects and preserve other informed risks.

Dependencies: See SD-005 through SD-010 for the initial campaign direction; exact mechanics, session length and power target still require design.

Next Action: Verify the baseline and design alternatives around the exploration/character targets in SD-009.

## SD-003 — Initial learning approach

Status: Superseded  
Date: 2026-10-23  
Category: Campaign setup

Decision: Run a bounded introductory adventure that can become a two-to-four-session mini-campaign; start with the verified core/Quickstart framework and at most one agreed change.

Reasoning: Provides a useful baseline and an easy continuation point.

Alternatives: Strict one-shot; immediate longer campaign; multiple homebrew systems from the start.

Tradeoffs: More sessions improve evidence but require scheduling; one session may reveal only immediate friction.

Dependencies: Q01–Q03, Q05–Q06, Q09–Q10 in [open questions](open-questions.md).

History (2026-10-23): This assistant proposal was never adopted. Noah selected at least four sessions and a large underground setting; SD-005 replaces its scope. The suggested limit of one change was also not accepted as a campaign constraint.

Next Action: Follow SD-005 and SD-009 rather than the original two-to-four-session proposal.

## SD-004 — Starting character level

Status: Accepted  
Date: 2026-10-23  
Category: Character setup

Authority: Noah's answer Q07, 2026-10-23.

Decision: Start characters at level 1 to experience learning and growth. Expanded capability is a separate design target in SD-009; this decision does not accept stock character rules unchanged.

Reasoning: Matches the entry point of the free pregens and starter adventure range.

Alternatives: Level 2–3 for more capability; level-0 gauntlet for an explicitly agreed different experience.

Tradeoffs: Fragility, creation time and progression expectations differ. More level-based capability does not automatically preserve the same encounter risk.

Dependencies: Player preferences, lethality, duration and chosen adventure.

History (2026-10-23): Changed from Proposed to Accepted after Noah explicitly selected level 1.

Next Action: Define capabilities and progression without silently changing starting level.

## SD-005 — Campaign scope and underground format

Status: Accepted  
Date: 2026-10-23  
Category: Campaign setup  
Authority: Noah's initial campaign setup answers.

Decision: Plan a short mini-campaign of at least four sessions, but not many more. Use approximately three players as a tentative planning assumption. The main location should feel like an enormous underground dungeon, temple, ruined city or Underdark-like environment. Include exploration, combat, social interaction and puzzles; no ranking or fixed ratios were supplied. No target date is set; session length and firm upper count remain open.

Reasoning: Supports an extended underground expedition with variety and room for character growth.

Alternatives: One-shot, smaller isolated dungeon, or a longer open-ended campaign.

Tradeoffs: The location can imply greater scale than the prepared area. Scope must remain bounded enough to conclude in a short run.

Dependencies: Session length, player experience, power package and final adventure choice.

Next Action: Develop a bounded playable route with optional branches; do not assume four-to-six sessions is already approved.

## SD-007 — Danger and alternative consequences

Status: Accepted  
Date: 2026-10-23  
Category: Failure and risk  
Authority: Noah's initial campaign setup answers.

Decision: Maintain real danger. Death remains possible, and death-alternative consequences may be appropriate. No universal rescue, automatic capture, death replacement or death quota is approved.

Reasoning: Supports high stakes without making every failure resolve identically.

Alternatives: Guaranteed character survival or treating every defeat as death.

Tradeoffs: Alternatives must follow the situation and retain meaningful consequences. Do not retroactively negate a stated risk or use prohibited content as a consequence.

Dependencies: Character durability, recovery rules, encounter design and private boundaries.

Next Action: Compare explicit situational consequence approaches after defining character power.

## SD-009 — Exploration flow and expanded character options

Status: Accepted  
Date: 2026-10-23  
Category: Homebrew priorities  
Authority: Noah's initial campaign setup answers.

Decision: Prioritize redesign of out-of-combat turn flow and expansion of features/abilities. Noah found exploration turns unsatisfying and sparse character features limiting without adding meaningful tension. Aim for level-1 characters with capability evocative of D&D 2024 while retaining many Shadowdark restrictions. This comparison is a design target, not approval to import D&D statistics, actions or classes.

Reasoning: Addresses the specific friction Noah identified rather than generic numerical power increases.

Alternatives: Unmodified play, numerical boosts alone, or wholesale D&D conversion.

Tradeoffs: More options can increase bookkeeping, bypass obstacles or displace roles; more actions/durability can alter danger independently. Exact exploration friction and the intended power dimensions need clarification.

Dependencies: SD-002, SD-004, source-backed rules review and the remaining questions.

Next Action: Compare exploration-flow and capability approaches, then draft complete proposals using the agreed rubric. No particular mechanic has been accepted.
