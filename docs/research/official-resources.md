## Coverage and update procedure

- Core, quickstart, sheets, one official builder, creator commentary, Cursed Scroll 1–3, solo rules, several adventures and Western Reaches anchors are covered.
- Monster, magic-item and random-table coverage is currently at publication level, not a copied database. Core and zine entries identify where these occur.
- Detailed advancement thresholds, complete ancestry/background/equipment lists, spell interactions, downtime procedures and revision comparisons require the legally obtained rules PDFs.
- Official creator videos are indexed at channel level plus one publisher-linked walkthrough; no claim of a complete video/Discord/FAQ archive.
- Licensed VTT implementations and translated editions have not received a systematic official-status audit.
- Publisher's 5E products are a separate line; do not label them Shadowdark resources merely because The Arcane Library sells them.
- Add new records with a unique R### ID. Preserve replaced URLs and access history where useful. Record publisher date separately from retrieval date. Compare exact files before asserting an option is identical across zines and Western Reaches.
