# Research provenance and limitations

Research date: 2026-10-23 (America/New_York).

## Method

Inspected supplied GitHub and Drive locations before mutation. Read the original README and main-branch metadata. Researched publisher landing/catalog/product pages, the official rules FAQ, creator commentary, character-sheet article and linked tools, license guidance and official Western Reaches campaign/reward pages. Used secondary sources only to identify explicitly unverified leads; no unauthorized PDF mirror was used as evidence or downloaded.

Evidence labels:
- **Confirmed fact:** directly supported by a retrieved first-party statement or audited project metadata.
- **Interpretation:** our explanation of design implications or project recommendation.
- **Unverified:** incomplete provenance, inaccessible body, untested tool function or unknown current revision/access.
A publication being official does not make every optional rule inside it part of this campaign. Creator preferences in a FAQ are not automatically printed rules.

## Completed initial work

The [catalog](official-resources.md) records 26 resources or component resources, including core/Quickstart, sheets, builder, Cursed Scroll 1–3 and their adventures, solo play, mini-adventures, community-sourced publisher material, newsletter offers, instruction, license and Western Reaches anchors.

Component adventures share their parent product's purchase requirement; do not count them as independent purchases. Print and PDF alternatives can overlap. Exact dates remain unknown where only a copyright year or shop listing is available.
