# Class-kit alternatives and weighted judgments

Status: Proposed; companion to the [complete-kit evaluation](class-kit-evaluation.md).  
Date: 2026-10-24 (UTC)  
No test results or adoption implied.

## Method

Criteria and weights: **A** agency/meaningful options 25%; **D** preservation of meaningful challenge 25%; **C** compatibility/balance 20%; **S** speed/simplicity 15%; **T** theme/campaign experience 15%.

Each criterion is scored 1–5. Weighted result = A×.25 + D×.25 + C×.20 + S×.15 + T×.15. These are provisional design judgments, not objective measurements, fairness rankings or win rates. Compatibility is confidence in adapting a direction to Shadowdark, not a claim that unwritten features are balanced.

For each class, **A/minimal** means the verified published Shadowdark baseline for the four core classes or a clearly identified original minimal stand-in for the other eight; **B/adapted** is the report's recommendation; **C/broad** imports the listed D&D subsystems. The broad option assumes more of those benefits are retained together and accordingly requires a larger redesign. No complete numerical versions of these alternatives exist yet.

## Sensitivity and limits

A 0.05 or 0.15 difference is too small to justify a confident claim of superiority. Wizard's narrow B-over-A lead is an explicit case for restraint. Several scores would fall if a prototype adds extra trackers, long decision pauses, automatic bypasses or frequent GM negotiation.

Recommended B scores assume explicit opportunity costs, actual successful uses, bounded utility and no universal numerical escalation of enemies. If retaining challenge requires canceling every new power, the design is failing both agency and theme. If a new power negates most resource pressure, its challenge and compatibility scores should fall.

Re-score concrete drafts after their numbers and interactions exist. Use observed choices, resource expenditure, encounter outcomes, failure consequences and preparation time rather than manufacturing precision from these preliminary ratings. No proposal moves to Testing or Accepted because it scores well.
