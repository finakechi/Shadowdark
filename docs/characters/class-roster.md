## Verified scope

The 2024 D&D Player's Handbook has twelve base classes: Barbarian, Bard, Cleric, Druid, Fighter, Monk, Paladin, Ranger, Rogue, Sorcerer, Warlock and Wizard.

Primary sources:
- [Official 2024 Basic Rules: Character Classes](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes).
- [Official Player's Handbook 2024 overview](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1810-updates-in-the-players-handbook-2024), published September 3, 2024; explicitly confirms twelve classes.

Noah selected this roster as our character-class design scope. This does not import D&D class mechanics, subclasses, species, backgrounds, feats, multiclassing or a twenty-level progression. Those need separate decisions. Artificer is outside this twelve-class PHB scope; this is not a claim about its availability in other publications.

## Original adaptation briefs — proposed

These are our design targets, not official Shadowdark or D&D rules. Signature ideas are sketches without finalized triggers, numerical effects or resource costs.

| Class | Defining decisions / first signature to draft | Contribution outside combat | Main interaction to constrain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barbarian | Commit to a dangerous stance: greater physical impact in exchange for exposure or reduced flexibility. | Force open routes, read immediate physical danger, endure hostile conditions. | Avoid becoming a tougher Fighter with equal control and no meaningful commitment. Rage must not be free damage plus broad damage resistance by default. |
| Bard | Choose whom to support and when: create an opening through performance, distraction or encouragement. | Lore, negotiation, rumors and bounded magical influence. | No universal expertise, automatic persuasion or constant bonuses to every ally. Social magic must state what targets perceive and what it cannot compel. |
| Cleric | Choose protection, restoration or spiritual intervention while competing for actions and resources. | Diagnose spiritual traces, interpret rites, temporarily contain a supernatural symptom. | No unlimited healing, effortless curse removal, safe-rest guarantee or certain divine answers. |
| Druid | Choose a bounded animal form or influence a living environment for a particular task. | Underground ecology, animal interaction, limited scouting and traversal. | Forms must specify equipment, senses and actions; no automatic fresh HP, free flight, perfect scouting or light independence. |
| Fighter | Trade some attack opportunity for positioning, protection or weapon control. | Assess defenses, organize difficult physical work, read signs of conflict. | Keep control conditional and useful; avoid repeatable enemy turn denial or a list that forbids ordinary improvised maneuvers. |
| Monk | Choose between striking, repositioning and controlling space through disciplined movement. | Balance, difficult movement, bodily discipline and access through awkward routes. | Mobility must not erase terrain; do not add a universal bonus-action system or multiply attacks merely to imitate D&D. |
| Paladin | Choose whom to protect and when to commit power to a decisive oath-driven intervention. | Recognize oath-related signs, negotiate obligations, confront supernatural corruption. | Do not combine a complete Fighter kit and complete Cleric casting. Oath terms need player-facing boundaries, not surprise GM punishment. |
| Ranger | Prepare or exploit a route, quarry or ambush situation. | Track inhabitants, identify expedition hazards, navigate unfamiliar underground terrain. | Useful in this dungeon, not only forests. No automatic navigation, free supplies, perfect tracking or encounter cancellation. |
| Rogue | Create and exploit openings through positioning, deception or mechanisms. | Infiltration, locks, traps, escape routes and criminal connections. | Expertise improves capability without monopolizing basic tasks. No automatic invisibility or trap immunity; damage should require a meaningful setup. |
| Sorcerer | Alter a bounded property of a spell through innate power, trading flexibility against a stated risk or resource. | Sense or manipulate a narrow magical phenomenon tied to the character's gift. | Distinguish spell shaping from Wizard breadth. No automatic Sorcery Point subsystem, unrestricted spell modification or free additional casting. |
| Warlock | Apply a narrow pact gift in different situations; choose when to invoke a more consequential boon. | Occult signs, strange contacts and bounded supernatural utility. | Repeatable magic cannot replace every weapon and tool. Pact terms must be explicit; avoid surprise patron penalties or debt tracked after every cast. |
| Wizard | Choose which learned method or spell suits a problem; preparation and acquired knowledge expand options. | Study magical structures, interpret arcane evidence, prepare bounded rituals. | Preserve learning and language competence. Avoid free comprehensive translation, perpetual light or universal magical obstacle removal. |

## Relationship to existing Shadowdark material

The supplied Quickstart verifies Fighter, Priest, Thief and Wizard. See the [baseline and printed page references](../rules/character-magic-baseline.md).

Accepted under SD-021: use **Cleric** as the roster name for our Priest-derived redesign and **Rogue** for our Thief-derived redesign. This is an approved naming/lineage mapping, not two extra classes, a mechanical conversion or a finding that their published rules are equivalent.

Existing official supplementary classes should be reviewed when legitimately accessible. The [official resource catalog](../research/official-resources.md) is the acquisition index. A verified product listing does not verify its interior mechanics. We have not completed an exhaustive official-equivalent audit for all twelve classes and must not invent their published rules.

## Distinction checks

- Fighter / Barbarian / Monk: control and loadout / dangerous commitment / movement and discipline.
- Rogue / Ranger / Bard: infiltration and exploitation / pursuit and preparation / influence and support.
- Cleric / Paladin / Druid: sacred intervention / oath and protection / forms and living environments.
- Wizard / Sorcerer / Warlock: learned breadth / innate spell shaping / narrow pact gifts.

These are primary emphases, not exclusive permissions. A Fighter may track, a Ranger may negotiate, and anyone may attempt plausible actions. A signature makes a class especially capable in a bounded way.

No mandatory healer, tank, scout or arcane caster is assumed for the tentative three-character party. Essential objectives need multiple approaches.
