## Recommended roadmap (not adopted campaign scope)

1. Scope: resolve group, time, tone, risk and one or two design goals.
2. Learn: read Quickstart; rehearse one exploration situation, one fight and one casting situation from the actual rules.
3. Select: compare one official starter dungeon, an original small site, and a hybrid. Match starting level and time.
4. Prepare: clear objective, situation, meaningful routes, NPC motives, hazards, rewards and exit conditions. Set a stopping point that can conclude or continue.
5. Run: brief players on danger and creative problem-solving; record rules friction without interrupting play.
6. Review: compare intended and actual risk, choices, pacing and spotlight; select next change.

## Candidate comparison

| Approach | Strength | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Quickstart's Lost Citadel | Free official learning package | Needs reading and a bounded objective to fit one session |
| Cursed Scroll 1's Hideous Halls | Level-1 exploration/faction candidate | Purchase/ownership check and extra setting material |
| Original small site | Precise fit to interests and time | More design and encounter-validation work |
| Official site with original hooks | Reduced prep with tailored motivations | Must separate module facts from our additions |

No encounter or entire dungeon is promised to fit a particular session length before page-level review.

## Playable-session readiness

The group has a clear objective and reason to cooperate; characters are legal for the agreed package; starting equipment is checked; the GM can run light, exploration, combat, magic and recovery; players know their options and risks; maps/handouts match their audience; the session has a viable conclusion or continuation point.
