## Recommended scope shape — not an adopted premise

Prepare a four-session route through a much larger underground complex, with optional branches for continuation. This is a preparation recommendation, not an agreed maximum count. The wider complex provides scale through inaccessible depths, distant lights, faction routes and architectural layers; only the next playable sections need full preparation.

A short arc should have a concrete objective and reachable conclusion, allowing the larger site to survive the campaign's end. Social interaction can come from inhabitants, rival expeditions, isolated communities and competing factions rather than requiring frequent returns to a surface town.

## Development order

1. Clarify session length, intended capability dimensions and exploration-turn friction.
2. Verify the corresponding baseline procedures in the uploaded guides.
3. Compare exploration-flow and character-option approaches in the [design brief](../rules/homebrew/design-brief.md).
4. Compare an official adventure, an original underground district and a hybrid against the chosen power package.
5. Prepare starting situation, objective, routes, NPC motives, warnings, rewards and consequences.
6. Run and review choices, spotlight, pacing, resource use and challenge.

No fixed ratio of combat to social play, required class composition, plot, level cap or automatic consequence rule has been assumed.
