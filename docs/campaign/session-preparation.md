# Session preparation

Use the [outline](campaign-outline.md); do not invent unresolved scope.

## Before selecting the adventure

- [ ] Resolve essential setup questions.
- [ ] Record chosen rulebook/Quickstart revision and official clarifications.
- [ ] Decide what success and failure mean for the session.
- [ ] Agree any homebrew test and rollback plan; link its exact HB version.
- [ ] Match adventure level, expected party and play length.

## Prepare the playable material

- [ ] Read the entire selected site and its exits, factions and rewards.
- [ ] Define one clear starting hook and objective; plan a shorter stopping point.
- [ ] Prepare character creation or pregens, equipment guidance and a spare-character approach if agreed.
- [ ] Explain checks, turns, light, gear, danger and creative actions in plain language.
- [ ] Prepare a light timer and a source-backed exploration/encounter procedure.
- [ ] Mark clues, warning signs, alternate routes, retreat and negotiation opportunities.
- [ ] Check enemy actions, numbers, morale, reactions and environmental advantages.
- [ ] Check treasure, magic utility and advancement assumptions.
- [ ] Prepare short NPC motives; avoid unnecessary setting history.
- [ ] Place GM maps in Drive 02 and player-safe assets in Drive 03.
- [ ] Confirm actual asset permissions and platform access.
- [ ] Prepare a short table expectations/boundaries check in appropriate private form.

## During play

- [ ] Track light and other relevant resources using agreed rules.
- [ ] Communicate observable danger; distinguish uncertainty from withheld essential information.
- [ ] Use consistent rulings; record temporary rulings separately from accepted homebrew.
- [ ] Note meaningful choices, pacing stalls and ability interactions.
- [ ] Conclude at the objective, an exit or a clear continuation point.

## After play

- [ ] Use the [session template](../../templates/session-notes.md).
- [ ] Ask what choices felt meaningful, what felt frustrating and what was unclear.
- [ ] Record playtest evidence and confounders.
- [ ] Update formal decisions only after Noah approves a change.
- [ ] Refresh status and prepare only the next playable scope.
